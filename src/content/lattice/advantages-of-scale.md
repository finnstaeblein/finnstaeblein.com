Cost-side advantages:

* Spreading fixed cost: fixed costs / unit falls, if you produce more units.
* Physics of scale: in a steel-tank volume grows as the cube, surface area as the square, so a bigger tank holds disproportionately more per pound of steel. This applies to ships, pipelines, warehouses etc.
* Experience: volume drives unit cost down.
* Bargaining power over suppliers
