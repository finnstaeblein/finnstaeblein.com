---
title: Advantages of Scale
theme: economics
summary: Cost, distribution, informational, and social-proof edges that grow with size.
date: 2026-10-21
---
Cost-side advantages:

* Spreading fixed cost: fixed costs / unit falls, if you produce more units.
* Physics of scale: in a steel-tank volume grows as the cube, surface area as the square, so a bigger tank holds disproportionately more per pound of steel. This applies to ships, pipelines, warehouses etc.
* Experience: volume drives unit cost down.
* Bargaining power over suppliers



Demand-side advantages:

* Network effects: the product gets more valuable as more people use it (e.g. social networks)
* Social proof: people buy what others are buying
* Brand 
* Advertising economies: historically, only big firms could afford national campaigns, which reinforced their scale. today, this looks different.



Others:

* Data flyweels: more users → more data → better product → more users
* Capital access
* Regulatory absorption that entrenches incumbents (Epic :)



When several of the above stack (especially network effects + social proof), markets tip to one dominant player. One example is newspapers: cities collapsed to a single dominant paper.
