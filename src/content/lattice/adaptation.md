Adaption is the process by which, over many generations, traits gets shaped to adapt to the environment an organism lives in. Selective pressure only acts on traits up until the end of the reproductive age of an organism (bc what happens to an organism after it can reproduce, has no influence on how much offspring it can produce / how excessively it can pass on it's genes).

Since adaptation is always to a specific environment, a trait that was advantageous can turn into a liability when conditions change. The same specialization that made an organism (or a company) dominant in one environemnt can be what leads to it's demise when the niche shifts.

Why it matters: durable advantage comes less from being well-adapted today than from being able to re-adapt faster than conditions change.
