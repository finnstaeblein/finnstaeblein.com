A reaction needs a disproportionate initial burst of energy before it will proceed on its own. Examples include striking a match or starting a combustion engine. 

Enzymes lower the activation energy required to start biological reactions in cells (which is great bc it allows said reactions to happen at body-temperatures). For example, lactase cleaves lactose into simpler sugars, glucose and galactose and in doing so lowers the energy barrier required to break the glycosidic bond between the two sugar molecules. Without lactase, breaking down lactose requires \~100 kj/mol of activation energy (i.e. the environment would need to be around 100 °C; lactase lowers the energy required to \~39 kj/mol).
