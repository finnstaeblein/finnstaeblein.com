cruelty can warrant beauty. the least it is supposed to do is end, end to make space.

space for beauty, or at least a different kind of cruelty.

this book is an endless slop of the exact same cruelty.

one grasps the meaning of the book in the first chapter. not even the mindless yuppies Ellis created in the book would be able to miss it. there is no surprise. no deeper meaning.

no resolve. no art. just continued terror.

this might be the worst book I have read.

the only thing I took away from the book is a repulsion against Patrick Bateman costumes
